Why can analysis take a few minutes?

This is real neural-network inference, not a simulated result. DICOM conversion is followed by MuscleMap segmentation. CPU inference can take tens of seconds per high-resolution stack. A GPU-backed inference host can substantially reduce this time.

What does this mean?

PVMQ normalizes paravertebral muscle T2 signal to CSF signal. In the source cohort, higher PVMQ was associated with PJK. This prototype does not convert an individual value into a validated PJK probability or universal risk category.

SOURCE STUDY CONTEXT

How should this result be interpreted?

Source cohort

272 adult spinal deformity patients; 87 (32.0%) developed PJK.

Study association

The PJK group had higher PVMQ and fat infiltration, with lower CSA and FCSA.

App limitation

No universal externally validated PVMQ cutoff or patient-specific PJK probability is applied.

Use this as a quantitative research marker together with segmentation QC and clinical context.

Method concordance

Aligned with manuscript

Axial T2 • provisional L1/2–L4/5 • bilateral multifidus/erector spinae • four-level averaging • L3 CSF normalization.

Implementation differences

This prototype uses automated MuscleMap contours and automated/provisional stack-level mapping rather than validated expert PACS measurements.

Research prototype. Automated contours and lumbar level mapping require human confirmation and external validation before clinical use.
