Why it matters

Mechanical risk is not a single-tissue problem.

The product is designed around the combined muscle–bone phenotype: muscle degeneration, fatty infiltration, vertebral bone quality and alignment are presented together instead of as isolated measurements.

Measurement definitions implemented in this prototype

Method-faithful
▦
PVMQ

Mean paravertebral muscle signal intensity across L1/2–L4/5 divided by L3 CSF signal intensity on axial T2 MRI.

▦
VBQ

Mean L1–L4 vertebral-body cancellous signal intensity divided by L3 CSF signal intensity on sagittal T1 MRI.

▦
CSA / FCSA / FI

Displayed as segmentation-derived metrics; the browser prototype does not fabricate these from uploaded images.
